## ARTICLE 2: ENGAGEMENT AND SERVICES

**2.1 Engagement.** Amazon hereby engages ProdCo, and ProdCo hereby accepts such engagement, to produce the Program in accordance with the terms of this Agreement. ProdCo shall render all production services necessary to complete the Program, including pre-production, principal photography, post-production, and delivery of final masters.

**2.2 Production Standards.** ProdCo shall produce the Program in a professional manner consistent with first-class production standards in the streaming entertainment industry. The Program shall be suitable for worldwide distribution on Amazon's platforms and shall comply with all applicable content guidelines provided by Amazon.

**2.3 Creative Consultation.** Amazon shall have meaningful consultation rights regarding all significant creative decisions, including casting, directing, writing, and production design. ProdCo shall consider Amazon's input in good faith, provided that Amazon shall have final approval over all elements designated in Exhibit E as requiring Amazon approval.

**2.4 Key Persons.** ProdCo shall ensure the continued attachment and active involvement of all Key Persons throughout production. If any Key Person becomes unavailable, ProdCo shall immediately notify Amazon, and Amazon may, in its sole discretion, approve a replacement, modify the Program, suspend production, or terminate this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 8: TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination for Convenience.** Amazon may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, in Amazon's sole and absolute discretion, upon written notice to ProdCo.

**8.2 Termination for Cause.** Amazon may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if ProdCo: (a) materially breaches any provision of this Agreement; (b) breaches any representation or warranty; (c) becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy; (d) is the subject of any governmental investigation relating to the Program; or (e) engages in conduct that brings Amazon into disrepute.

**8.3 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination for any reason: (a) all rights granted herein shall remain vested in Amazon; (b) ProdCo shall immediately deliver all Materials to Amazon; (c) Amazon shall own all work in progress; and (d) ProdCo shall have no right to any reversion of rights. Upon termination for convenience, Amazon shall pay ProdCo for services rendered and costs properly incurred through the termination date. Upon termination for cause, Amazon shall have no obligation to make any further payments.

**8.4 No ProdCo Termination.** ProdCo shall not have the right to terminate this Agreement for any reason. ProdCo's sole remedy for any breach by Amazon shall be an action for monetary damages subject to the limitations set forth herein.

**8.5 Survival.** The following provisions shall survive termination: Articles 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, and any other provision that by its nature should survive.

**9.1 Confidentiality Obligations.** ProdCo shall maintain in strict confidence and shall not disclose to any third party any Confidential Information of Amazon, including the terms of this Agreement, Amazon's business strategies, NPI, and any other information designated as confidential by Amazon.

**9.2 Program Confidentiality.** ProdCo shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all creative elements of the Program, including scripts, storylines, casting decisions, and production details. ProdCo shall implement and enforce security protocols including watermarked script distribution, on-set device restrictions, and personnel confidentiality agreements.

**9.3 Permitted Disclosures.** ProdCo may disclose Confidential Information only to employees, contractors, and agents with a need to know who are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations. ProdCo may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, provided ProdCo gives Amazon prompt notice and cooperates with any protective order.

**9.4 Breach Notification.** ProdCo shall immediately notify Amazon upon becoming aware of any actual or suspected unauthorized disclosure and shall cooperate with Amazon's investigation and remediation efforts.

**9.5 Survival.** Confidentiality obligations shall survive for five (5) years after termination, except that trade secret obligations shall survive indefinitely.
